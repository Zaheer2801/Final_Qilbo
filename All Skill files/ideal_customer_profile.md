# Ideal Customer Profile

> This file directly informs procurement, pricing, and product recommendations. Read it
> every time before making a stocking or trend recommendation. Update it as real sales data
> accumulates — early entries below are the Owner's initial observations, not yet confirmed
> by a full sales history, and should be labeled as such until enough data backs them up.

## Neighborhood / customer base
- Store is situated in a neighborhood with a majority Jamaican and Mexican / Latin American
  customer base, with a Black community presence as well.
- **Confidence level: Owner observation, ~1 month of data.** Treat as a working hypothesis
  to refine, not settled fact — flag this qualifier when using it to justify a procurement
  decision until sales data confirms it.

## Observed purchasing patterns (as of initial setup)
- **Fast movers:** Tequila, vodka.
- **Also fast-moving:** Remy Martin, Hennessy — specifically in small formats (50ml, 100ml,
  200ml, 375ml).
- **Slow movers (same brands, larger format):** 750ml bottles of the above — they sell, but
  much less frequently than the minis.
- **Interpretation to apply carefully:** small-format velocity suggests single-serve /
  on-the-go purchasing behavior, possibly gifting around Hennessy/Remy Martin. This is a
  working theory — Buddie should note it as a theory when referencing it, not state it as
  confirmed customer behavior.

## What Buddie should NOT do with this file
- Do not extrapolate specific ethnic or cultural product preferences beyond what's recorded
  here from actual sales data. No inventing categories, holidays, or preferences not
  observed or explicitly told to Buddie by the Owner.
- Do not use this file as the sole basis for a large reorder — cross-check against actual
  velocity data in the inventory system (see `lead-team.md`'s "Demand-driven procurement
  process" section).

## Update protocol
Once 2–3 months of real sales data exist, this file should be rewritten from that data
rather than the initial observations above. Move superseded entries to `memory.md` under
"superseded assumptions" rather than deleting them outright, so the reasoning trail is kept.
